Our active Debating Club held its termly inter-class showcase this week, and the standard was remarkable. Pupils argued motions on the environment, community service, and the importance of reading — thinking on their feet and listening respectfully to one another.

Public speaking builds confidence, critical thinking, and mutual respect. We are proud to give every child a chance to find their voice.

Well done to all our debaters and their patient coaches.
