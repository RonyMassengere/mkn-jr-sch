MUKONO JUNIOR SCHOOL
ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS CHECKLIST

We are pleased to guide your family through a clear and welcoming admissions
process. Contact the admissions office before your visit to confirm the
requirements for your child's class.

DOCUMENTS TO PREPARE
1. A completed student application form.
2. The child's birth certificate or other official proof of age.
3. Recent passport-size photographs.
4. The most recent school report or transfer letter, where applicable.
5. An immunisation or health record for a pupil joining from another school.
6. Parent or guardian identification and current contact details.

BEFORE ENROLMENT
- Bring original documents for verification and keep copies for your records.
- Share relevant medical, allergy, or learning-support information with the
  admissions team.
- Confirm class placement, current fees, uniform, and scholastic requirements
  with the school before making purchases.
- Arrange a guided campus visit and agree on the next steps with the team.
