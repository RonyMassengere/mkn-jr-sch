MUKONO JUNIOR SCHOOL
FEE PLANNING GUIDE

The current fee schedule is published by class and study arrangement on the
Academics page. Families can also download the latest available schedule from
the Admissions page.

Before enrolment or the start of a new term, please:
- confirm the current charges for your child's class and study arrangement;
- check which learning materials and requirements are included;
- ask the school office about uniform and additional scholastic needs; and
- discuss payment arrangements and the school's communication channels.

Published figures are subject to confirmation by the school office. Contact
the admissions team if you would like help understanding the current fee
schedule or planning for your child's start.
