Mukono Junior School Fee Guide

School fees are communicated clearly to families before each term begins. The exact amount may vary depending on the class level and whether the child is a day scholar or boarder.

Parents are encouraged to:
- confirm the applicable class-level charges,
- ask about required learning materials and uniforms,
- discuss payment timing and school communication channels before the term starts.

The school office is available to share a transparent fee profile and answer questions about school expenses.
