We are bursting with pride! Mukono Junior School has been crowned **Mukono District Champions** in the FUFA Primary Schools Championship (Odilo) after a dominant run through the local qualifiers.

Our pupils showed discipline, teamwork, and heart in every match — the very values we celebrate both on and off the field. A huge thank you to our coaches, teachers, parents, and the whole community who came out to cheer.

> "These children played with courage and respect. This is what Mukono Junior School is about." — Head Coach

Congratulations to every player and supporter. Onward to the regional stage!
